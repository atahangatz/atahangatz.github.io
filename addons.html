TAPE 01 · NOW PLAYINGBLENDER ADD-ON

Blender says 8. Your engine says 24.

Polygraph is a Blender add-on that shows the vertex count your game engine will really get, checks it against a real platform budget, and tells you what to cut first.

1.0 comes out December 8 for Blender 4.2 and later.

Read the guide ▶ Pick an edition

What it does

  • The vertex count glTF, Unity and Unreal will really load, live in the sidebar and in the viewport, with what each UV seam and sharp edge adds.
  • A verdict against a real budget: Meta Quest, every VRChat rank, Roblox, Fortnite UEFN, visionOS, Second Life, The Sims 4 and more, or limits you type in.
  • Where to Cut: the changes that would save the most, each with its cause and what it saves.
  • What If, in Pro: assume a cut, a lower LOD or a triangle cap before touching a mesh, and see the verdict move.
  • The player's view, in Pro: judge only what the camera sees, and see a model at the size the player will.
  • 21 mesh checks, marked on the object and selectable in Edit Mode.
  • Reports to hand over: HTML in every edition, with JSON, CSV and Markdown in Pro.
  • In Studio, the same check on a build server from the command line, with JUnit results for CI.

Editions

Every edition is GPL, so every one of them can be used for paid work. No account, no licence key, and it works offline.
