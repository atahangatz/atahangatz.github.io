Blender says 8. Your engine says 24.

A default cube has 8 vertices in Blender. Send it to glTF, Unity or Unreal and it arrives with 24, because each corner splits once for every face that meets it at a hard edge or a UV seam.

Polygraph counts what the engine will count, checks the total against a budget for the platform you’re making for, and points at what to cut first.

Free shows you the problem. Pro helps you fix it and plan around it. Studio does the same for a team of five.

1.0 comes out December 8 for Blender 4.2 and later.
